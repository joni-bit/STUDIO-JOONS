About me

Hi, I’m Joni. A 25-year-old designer with a degree in Communication and Multimedia Design from Hogeschool Utrecht.

In my free time you’ll probably find me in the gym. I also like to get creative with digital design and painting.

I’m a very social person and enjoy working with others. As a native Dutch speaker who is also fluent in English, I’m comfortable collaborating in international teams.
