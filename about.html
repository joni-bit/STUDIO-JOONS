About me

I’m Joni, a creative designer and concepter. I love getting to know a brand so well that I can feel how far it stretches, and then finding something new inside it: a pickle with attitude, a potato pin-up, a radio studio you can colour in.

I’m at my best in a team. Give me a tough brief and a group of people to bounce ideas off, and I’m happy. I love working on things that really reach people, and making them smile, with my work or just by being me.

Outside work you’ll find me in the gym, out running (I’m training for my first half marathon), cooking Italian or Nigerian food, or planning the next trip from my very long list of cities. Next up: Milan.
